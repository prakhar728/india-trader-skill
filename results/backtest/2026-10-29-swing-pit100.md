## Rules

- **momentum_rotation** (monthly momentum rotation): At the last close of each month rank every stock above its 200-day SMA by its 12-1 month return (close 21 sessions ago / close 252 sessions ago; variant 6-1: 126 sessions); hold the top 10 (variants 5, 20). On the first trading day of the next month sell, at the open, what left the top N, and buy the newcomers with equity / N each. Holdings that stay are not re-weighted. No stops. Ranked by the momentum itself.
- **ma_cross** (50/200-day moving-average cross): The 50-day SMA closes above the 200-day SMA after being at or under it the day before -> buy the next open. Exit at the next open after the 50-day closes back under the 200-day. Variants 20/100 and 50/150. Ranked by strongest 6-month return first.
- **oversold_bounce_d** (daily oversold bounce in an uptrend): Close above its 200-day SMA (the trend is still up) and RSI(14, Wilder) under 30 (variants 25, 35) -> buy the next open. Exit at the next open after RSI(14) closes above 50, or after 15 sessions (variant 10). No stop. Ranked by lowest RSI(14) first.
- **high_52w** (52-week high on volume): Close above the highest close of the 252 sessions before it, on volume at least 1.5x its 50-day average (variants 2x, no volume filter) -> buy the next open. Exit at the next open after a close below the 50-day SMA (variant 20-day). Ranked by highest volume multiple first.
- **gap_volume_drift** (gap-up on heavy volume, then drift (results proxy)): A proxy for post-results drift (no historical results dates here): the day opens at least 4 % above the previous close (variant 3 %), trades at least 3x its 50-day average volume and closes in the top half of its range -> buy the next open. Stop at the gap day's low (skipped if the next open is already under it). Hold 20 sessions (variants 10, 40), out at the next open. Ranked by highest volume multiple first.
- **bull_flag_d** (daily bull flag breakout): india-scanner's bull flag at yesterday's close (up more than 10 % over the 10 sessions before the last 5, then the last 5 closes inside a 5 % range); today closes above the flag's highest high -> buy the next open. Stop at the flag's lowest low; target 2R from the fill (variant 3R); else out at the next open after 20 sessions (variant 10). Ranked by the biggest pole (the 10-session run) first.
- **breakout_20d** (20-day closing-high breakout): Close above the highest close of the 20 sessions before it (variant 55) -> buy the next open (india-scanner's breakout_20d). Trailing exit: the next open after a close below the lowest low of the 10 sessions before it (variant 20). No other stop, no time limit. Ranked by strongest 6-month return first (relative strength).
- **pullback_rsi2** (RSI(2) pullback in an uptrend): Close above its 200-day SMA and RSI(2, Wilder) under 10 (variants 5, 15) -> buy the next open. Exit at the next open after a close above the 5-day SMA, or after 10 sessions. No stop by default (variant: a stop 3 x ATR(14) under the signal close). Ranked by lowest RSI(2) first (the deepest pullback).

## Caveats

- POINT-IN-TIME universe (pit100): at each month-end, series-EQ stocks (INE ISIN, >= 200 sessions of history, close >= Rs 20) ranked by median daily traded value over ~6 months; the top 100 are buyable on signals until the next month-end; exits are always allowed. 285 stocks were members at some point in 2015-2026; 20 of them stopped trading before the end (delisted, merged, suspended) and stay in for the months they qualified. This is a LIQUIDITY universe, not the official Nifty index history (index reconstitutions also weigh free float and F&O eligibility), so it is close to, not identical with, the Nifty 100.
- DELISTINGS: a position whose stock stops trading is sold at its LAST available close (2 such exits across the default runs). That is fair for a merger or buy-out but OPTIMISTIC for a company that failed or was suspended (a real holder might have sold far lower, or not at all).
- Prices are NSE's own bhavcopy OHLC (unadjusted; its PREVCLOSE is NOT adjusted on ex-dates either), back-adjusted for bonuses, splits, consolidations, rights and demergers listed in NSE's corporate-action (Bc) files: the purpose's exact ratio when the ex-day gap agrees, else the gap snapped to a clean ratio; rights / demergers by the ex-day open (or close) / last close; Yahoo's split events where no Bc file listed the action (245 listed adjustments in 149 stocks of this universe). Checked against Yahoo's split-adjusted closes: 92 % of the 483 stocks both have line up with no level step > 3 %; the rest are demergers Yahoo leaves unadjusted, Yahoo's own year-boundary errors and small rights issues. A few small-cap actions are still missed (e.g. AGIIL 2025-02-07, DWARKESH 2017-08-10 look like unlisted splits; some rights issues). NOT dividend-adjusted: returns are PRICE returns, like the Nifty 50 price index (^NSEI); NIFTYBEES (the ETF) includes reinvested dividends less expenses. Benchmarks are Yahoo's, bought on 2014-12-31's close, no costs.
- Costs: nse.delivery_charges, Dhan delivery (CNC): Rs 0 brokerage; STT 0.1 % on buy and sell; NSE txn 0.0030699 %; SEBI Rs 10/crore; stamp 0.015 % on the buy; GST 18 % on brokerage + txn + SEBI + DP; DP Rs 12.50 + GST per scrip per sell day (pricing pages checked 2026-10-29; today's schedule applied to the whole history). Slippage 0.10 % a side for stocks then in the PIT top 100, 0.25 % for the rest. Taxes are NOT modelled (STCG / LTCG). Not tax advice.
- Fills: signals on the close, entries at the next open + slippage; stops on the day's low, a gap through the stop at the open; close-based and time exits at the next open. No circuit limits, no liquidity check beyond index membership; a 20 % upper circuit could make an entry impossible in real life.
- Portfolio: Rs 1,00,000, at most 10 positions, equal sizing, compounding; cash earns 0 (a liquid fund would add ~6 % a year on idle cash, which favours low-exposure strategies here).
- Walk-forward: variants chosen on 2015-01-01 to 2021-12-31 (best Sharpe), reported on 2022-01-03 to 2026-10-29 only. Sharpe uses daily returns, rf = 0.
- A backtest is not a forecast and never places an order. Not financial advice.
