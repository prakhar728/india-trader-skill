- **nifty_vert_0dte** (options-credit): On expiry day the whole time value of a Nifty weekly option goes to zero by 15:30. The buyers of cheap out-of-the-money expiry-day options are mostly individuals (SEBI's 2024 study: 91 % of individual F&O traders lose, most of them option buyers) paying a lottery premium for a large move in a few hours; the earlier backtest of that very trade (buy ATM on an ORB) lost 4 % of premium at mid prices on expiry days before costs. The mirror image, hedged: sell a vertical k x sigma away (sigma = India VIX scaled to the trading time left), long wing 100 points further, and let the exchange settle it. The 09:30 put spread harvests the index put skew (institutional hedging demand keeps puts dear); the ORB variant sells the side the first 15 minutes moved away from, so the seller takes the ORB buyer's side.

## Caveats

- Survivorship: universes are today's NSE index lists applied to the past.
- Yahoo keeps 60 days of 5-minute bars: 60 sessions split 36 / 12 / 12 is a short history; the gates are deliberately strict because of it.
- Fills: decision bar's close +/- slippage (1 tick; 2 in the cost stress), stops and flat-by exits worse by slippage, gaps through the stop at the open, targets exact. Real fills in fast markets can be worse.
- Charges: Dhan's intraday equity schedule (nse.charges); shorts pay STT on the entry (the sell) and stamp duty on the cover (the buy). The cost stress multiplies charges by 1.5.
- Every parameter combination tried counts as a trial, and so does every side look logged with `research.py note --looks N`; the Deflated Sharpe Ratio uses the running total, and the report re-states every gate's DSR at today's total.
- Portfolio cap (runs from 2026-10-29 on): at most the capital (Rs 1,00,000) deployed and 5 intraday positions at once, signals beyond it skipped in decision-time order; earlier run files were made without it and their account-level totals (total Rs, daily t, benchmark) assume unlimited capital.
- No result here is a promise of returns. Research never places an order. Not financial advice.
